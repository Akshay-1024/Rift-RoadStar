Rift Roadstar Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026

Rift Roadstar is a mobile game made by Akshay. This policy explains what data the game uses and why. The game has no accounts, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress is saved on your phone: scores, coins, upgrades, missions and settings. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, including optional video ads that give in-game rewards. To show ads and measure them, Google may collect and process data from your device, such as:

  • your advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location
  • device and app information
  • how you interact with ads
  • crash and performance data from the ads software

Google handles this data under its own policies. You can read how Google uses data at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, and its Privacy Policy.

Your choices

If you are in a region that requires consent, such as the European Economic Area or the UK, the game asks for your choice before showing personalized ads. You can change that choice under Privacy Choices in the game's Settings.

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or limit personalized ads, in your phone's settings under Privacy and Ads.

Sharing your score

The Share button opens your phone's own share menu with a short message about your score. You choose the app and the people to send it to. The game does not send that message anywhere itself.

Internet use

The game uses the internet to load ads. The game itself can be played without an internet connection, but ad rewards will not be available.

Children

Rift Roadstar is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
